Quick Definitions
Bullish: upward directional bias.
Bearish: downward directional bias.
Ranging: price oscillates between identifiable areas.
Consolidating: compressed movement before a possible expansion.
Mixed: signals do not clearly agree.

How the Profit Factor Mode Works

PF = Gross Profit ÷ Gross Loss. If PF = 2.0, the strategy historically/model-wise generates about R2 of gross profit for every R1 of gross loss, before costs and execution effects.
